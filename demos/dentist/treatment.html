Is it for you?

Good for the things most adults want to fix

Aligners work best for mild to moderate problems. We’ll tell you honestly at the consultation if something else would do a better job.

Crowding

Front teeth that overlap or twist, which also makes them harder to clean.

Gaps

Small spaces between teeth, including a gap at the front that’s opened up over the years.

Teeth that have moved

You had braces as a teenager, stopped wearing your retainer, and things have drifted.

When we’ll suggest something else: big bite problems, or teeth that need to move a long way, are usually better with fixed braces. If that’s you, we’ll say so and refer you to an orthodontist we trust. For under-18s, ask about an NHS orthodontic referral at their check-up.

Questions

What people ask us

Anything else, ring Delroy on 01632 960 214 or ask at your check-up.

Does it hurt?

Not really. Each new set feels tight for a day or two, a bit like a pressure ache, and then settles. Most people put a new set in before bed so they sleep through the worst of it.

Will people notice?

Most won’t, unless they’re very close. The small bumps we add are tooth-coloured. You might lisp slightly for the first couple of days while your tongue gets used to them.

Can I drink coffee with them in?

Take them out for anything except cold water. Coffee and tea stain the trays, and anything sugary sits against your teeth. Rinse or brush before you put them back.

What if I lose one?

Ring us. Usually you move on to the next set early or go back to the last one while we make a replacement. The first replacement is included; after that it’s £95.

How is this different from mail-order aligners?

A dentist examines you in person before anything is planned, checks your gums and existing fillings, and sees you every eight weeks. If a tooth isn’t moving as it should, we spot it early and adjust the plan.

Can I have aligners on the NHS?

Not as an adult: the NHS doesn’t fund aligners for cosmetic straightening. Under-18s with a significant need may qualify for NHS braces through a referral, and we’ll check at their NHS check-up.
