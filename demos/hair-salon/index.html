    Since 2017, off the Shore

    An old chandler’s shop, five chairs and no hard sell

    Morven Laird cut hair in the West End for fifteen years before she took the keys to a former ship chandler’s on Chandlers Wynd. She painted it the colour of the old shopfront, put in five chairs and kept the big window.

    We’re a small team, and we’d rather tell you the truth than sell you a treatment. If your hair won’t do what the photo does, we’ll say so, and we’ll suggest something that will.
