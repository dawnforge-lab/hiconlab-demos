About us

Mick, Jas and the crew

Mick started a window round in Bedminster in 1998 with a ladder, a bucket and a bike trailer. Jas joined in 2017 after six years as a lab technician, put the first pure-water van on the road and taught herself soft washing, which is what Bristol’s painted render really needs.

There are four of us now. We’re still the people who come to your house.

  • Mick HollisWindows and the round. Knows which gate sticks on every street.
  • Jas HollisRender, roofs and the soft wash mix. Runs the business side.
  • Dev OkaforDriveways, patios and solar panels.
