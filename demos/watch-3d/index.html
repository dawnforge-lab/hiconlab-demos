The P39First batch of 150

Made in Prescot. Again.

For two centuries the movements inside English watches were made in Prescot. The P39 is the first since the 1950s to be designed, made and finished here again, at our workshop on Atherton Street.

IIThe movement

Turn it over.

Calibre FW-1 is built the English way, under a frosted gilt three-quarter plate, with its jewels set in gold chatons and held by blued screws. We cut, finish and assemble every one in Prescot, and the sapphire back lets you watch it work.

  • 25jewels
  • 28,800beats an hour
  • 68hours of reserve

    IVDial and strap

    Three dials. Three straps.

    Each dial is brass, brushed in a sunburst from the centre and lacquered six times, so it goes from bright to deep as the light moves across it. Choose one, then a strap to go with it.
