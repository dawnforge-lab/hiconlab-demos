    First visit

    Your first session, start to finish

    1. Pick a class marked “good first session”

      Lifting Foundations, Kettlebell Basics, Circuits, Boxfit, Mobility, Strong at Sixty and Women’s Lifting Club all suit someone who has never trained. It’s free, whichever you choose.

    2. Come ten minutes early

      Say hello at the front desk. Your coach will show you the changing rooms, the free lockers and the water fountain.

    3. Go at your own pace

      Every move has an easier version and nobody is timing you. Tell the coach about injuries or worries; it stays between the two of you.

    4. No sales pitch afterwards

      If you’d like to come back, pay £9 a class or look at a membership. If not, that’s fine too.

    Show me good first sessions

    Classes

    Five kinds of class

    Every class is coached and capped, from first-timer lifting to boxing on the pads. Each one has an easier version of every move.

    • Strength

      Barbell Club · Lifting Foundations · Women’s Lifting Club · Strong at Sixty

      Squats, deadlifts and presses, coached properly. Foundations starts with an empty bar; Strong at Sixty uses chairs and boxes, with no getting down on the floor.

    • Kettlebells

      Kettlebells · Kettlebell Basics

      Swings, squats and carries. Forty-five minutes and you’ve worked everything.

    • Circuits

      Circuits

      Ten stations, music on, a bit of puffing. Every station has an easier and a harder version.

    • Boxing

      Boxfit · Boxing Technique

      Pads, bags and skipping with Dele. No sparring: nobody hits you back.

    • Mobility

      Mobility & Stretch

      Slow work for stiff hips, backs and shoulders. Good after a day at a desk, or a night shift.

    About us

    A small gym, run by its coaches

    Nadia Parekh and Tom Rennie opened Kingfield in 2022, in an old print workshop off the canal. Nadia had taught PE for nine years; Tom had spent eleven as a leisure-centre instructor.

    They wanted a gym where classes are small enough for the coach to notice you, prices are written down plainly, and nobody feels out of place, whether you’re seventeen or seventy.

    Most of our members had never set foot in a gym before. Start with a free session and see if it suits you.

    “Half our members started in Lifting Foundations with an empty bar. Now they help the new people.”

    Nadia Parekh, co-owner and head coach
