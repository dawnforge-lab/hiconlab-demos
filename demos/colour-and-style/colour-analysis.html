How It Works

During our session, we’ll consider your genetics, your temperament, and the results of the draping process to tailor every colour to enhance your natural beauty and vibe. To dive deeper into your temperament, we’ll complete a fun temperament quiz to ensure your colours match your personality as well as your appearance.

  1. 1

    Introduction to Seasonal Colour Theory

    Learn how the seasonal method works and what makes your palette unique.

  2. 2

    Temperament Quiz

    Complete a quiz that helps align your colours with your inner vibe and personality.

  3. 3

    Draping Session & Analysis

    Experience a full draping to identify your dominant colours and see which shades lift your mood and look best.

  4. 4

    Follow-Up & Colour Fan Delivery

    Receive a personalised digital colour fan to guide your shopping and styling.

After our one-on-one session, you’ll feel confident about your colours and know how to style them for every occasion.

You’ll walk away with a colour digital fan to reference when shopping, so you’ll never make a mistake again. Plus, you’ll receive a follow-up message with all the information we discussed during your session.
