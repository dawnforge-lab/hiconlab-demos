The MOT fee

Why our MOT is under the legal maximum.

The government sets the most a test centre can charge for a car MOT: £54.85. We charge £45, or £35 when you book it with a service, because the car is already on the lift.

If it fails and you leave it with us for the repairs, the retest is free. Nia or Tomasz will show you exactly what failed and give you a price before anything is fixed.

MOT fees carry no VAT, so the price you see is the price you pay. You can test up to a month (less a day) before the due date and keep the same date for next year.

Questions

Things people ask Bev.

Will a service here affect my car’s warranty?

No. You can have a car under warranty serviced at an independent garage, as long as the work follows the maker’s schedule and uses parts of matching quality. We do both, and stamp the book.

What happens if you find something else?

We ring you, tell you what it is and what it costs, and send a photo if it helps. You say yes or no. Nothing extra goes on the bill without a yes.

How and when do I pay?

When you collect the car, by card or cash. There’s nothing to pay when you book.

Do you work on electric cars?

Hybrids, yes: Tomasz is qualified on hybrid systems. Fully electric cars, not yet. Ring us and we’ll point you to someone good.

Do you have a courtesy car?

No, but we’re five minutes’ walk up Coton Hill from Shrewsbury station, and a short walk from town. Most people drop off on the way to work and we text when it’s ready.
