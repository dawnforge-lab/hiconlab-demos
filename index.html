Notes — About these demos

Made to be clicked, not just looked at.

  1. N1

    Each demo is a small, self-contained build that runs in your browser. There’s no live AI and nothing is sent anywhere: the answers, bookings and orders are scripted.

  2. N2

    The videos are recorded from the demos themselves, with a scripted cursor, so what you watch is what you can try.

  3. N3

    Cards marked “In progress” are still being built. Their videos and demos will appear here when they’re ready.

Notes — What’s real

Fictional businesses, real work.

The shops, garages, salons and gyms in these demos don’t exist, and neither do their customers, staff, prices or reviews. Phone numbers come from the ranges Ofcom keeps for drama.

Two cards are different. Kreativehub and The Colour and Style are design recreations of real sites, made for portfolio purposes and labelled on their cards.
