Every postcode we cover has its own day of the week, so your windows are done on the same day every four or eight weeks and bigger jobs fit in around the round.

On your round day

What happens on the day

A text the evening before

Kerry sends it around six, so you can leave the side gate unlocked or tell us to skip the back.

We arrive in our window

Two-hour arrival times for visits and big jobs. Window cleans just happen during the day; you don’t need to be in.

Rain doesn’t stop us

Pure water leaves nothing behind for rain to stick to, so we clean in drizzle. In high winds we come back later in the week.

Pay when we’re done

A card link by text after each clean, or a monthly bank transfer if you’d rather. No contracts: pause or stop whenever you like.

Questions

Things people ask Kerry

Do I need to be in?

Not for windows, gutters, render or the outside of anything. For a back garden behind a locked gate, leave it unlocked or tell us where the key is. We only need you in for the free visit if you want to talk it through.

I’m just outside your area. Will you still come?

For one-off jobs, yes, if you’re in BS2, BS5, BS6, BS7, BS9, BS31 or BS48: we come out on Saturdays for anything from a window clean to a roof. We don’t run window rounds there, because we couldn’t be on time for you every four weeks.

Can I pause my round over winter?

Yes. Text Kerry and we’ll skip you until you say. Most people keep going, because January is when the glass gets worst.

Do your prices include VAT?

Totals do. We’re VAT registered, so the guide-price ticket lists the work ex VAT, adds VAT at 20% and shows the total you pay, so you can see it add up. The “from” prices and the round price are shown with VAT included.

Is soft washing safe for plants and pets?

We wet the plants first, rinse them after, and keep pets inside while we spray. The biocide breaks down within a few days. Jas will tell you exactly what’s in it if you ask.
