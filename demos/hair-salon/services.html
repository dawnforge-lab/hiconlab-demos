Good to know

Before you book

Colour

Skin tests

If you’re new to colour with us, or it’s been more than six months, we need a quick skin test at least 48 hours before. Pop in any time we’re open. It takes two minutes and it’s free.

Changes

Cancelling or running late

Give us 24 hours’ notice and there’s no charge. Less than that and we may ask for half the price next time. Running more than 15 minutes late? Call us and we’ll see what we can still do.

Paying

Pay on the day

No deposit to book. Card or cash when you leave. If something takes longer than planned, we’ll ask before it changes the price, not after.

Questions

The things people ask at the desk

How do I know my hair length?

Short is above your chin. Shoulder is between your chin and your shoulders. Long is anything past your shoulders. If you’re between two, pick the longer one; if it turns out shorter, we charge the shorter price.

Do you cut curly and Afro hair?

Yes. Femi specialises in curly and coily hair and cuts it dry, curl by curl. Morven also does curly cuts. Silk presses are Femi only.

Can I bring a photo?

Please do. Bring a few, and one of something you don’t want. We’ll tell you honestly how close we can get with your hair.

Do you do men’s cuts?

We price by hair, not by who’s wearing it. Ross does skin fades and short back and sides; anyone with longer hair books the same cuts as everyone else.

Can my child sit in with me?

Of course. There’s a box of books by the window. Under-12s cuts are £20 with Ross, Isla or Femi.
