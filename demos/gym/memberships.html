    The small print, in plain English

    No catches. If something here isn’t clear, ask at the desk and we’ll rewrite it.

    No joining fee

    You pay for the month you’re in and nothing else. No admin fee, no fob fee.

    No minimum term

    Join for one month if you like. Most people stay; nobody has to.

    Leaving

    Email hello@dryside.example or tell us at the desk. Your membership ends one month after you tell us, so you’ll make at most one more payment.

    Pausing

    Up to two months a year, free, for any reason. Tell us before your next payment date.

    Price changes

    We’ll give you two months’ notice of any change, so you can leave before it applies.

    Missed payments

    We’ll text you and try again after five days. There’s no late fee.

    Questions people ask at the desk

    Can I try it before I join?

    Yes. Your first session is free: any class marked “good first session” on the timetable. There’s no sales pitch afterwards.

    Are there changing rooms and showers?

    Yes, in the old baths changing rooms, with hot showers, free lockers (no coin needed) and an accessible changing room with its own shower.

    Can under-16s come?

    The gym and classes are for 16 and over. Fourteen and fifteen-year-olds can come to Saturday Circuits with a parent who is also taking part.

    I have an injury or a health condition. Can I still come?

    Almost always. Tell us when you book and the coach will plan around it. If you’ve had recent surgery or your GP has told you to take it easy, check with them first. Strong at Sixty and Mobility & Stretch are good places to start.

    When is it quiet?

    Weekdays between 10:00 and 16:00 are quiet, which is why off-peak is cheaper. It’s busiest from 17:00 to 19:30 on weekdays.

    Not sure yet? Your first session is free

    Pick any class marked “good first session” this week. Come ten minutes early and we’ll show you round.
