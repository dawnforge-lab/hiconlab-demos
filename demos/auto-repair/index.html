How we work

Three things we always do.

  1. We ring before any extra work

    If we find something that isn’t on the job card, you get a call with a price. You decide. Nothing extra goes on the bill without a yes.

  2. We show you what we found

    Worn pads, a split boot, a weeping shock: we text you a photo, or walk you under the car if you’re here.

  3. You get the old parts back

    Whatever we replace goes in a box in your boot, unless you’d rather we recycled it. Then you know it was done.

About us

Hands on the wheel at ten to two.

Alun Pryce opened in the old coachworks in 1994 and named it after the first thing he told every apprentice. Nia was one of those apprentices. She has run the place since 2019, and Alun still comes in on Thursdays for the electrical faults nobody else can find.

  • Nia

    Nia Hughes

    Owner and MOT tester

    Started here as an apprentice in 2006. Does most of the MOTs and all of the awkward conversations.

  • Alun

    Alun Pryce

    Founder, diagnostics

    Thursdays only, by his own rules. If a warning light has beaten two other garages, it goes to Alun.

  • Ellie

    Ellie Morgan

    Apprentice, tyres

    In her second year at college. Will tell you honestly whether your tyres have another winter in them.

Also on the team: Tomasz our hybrid-qualified technician, and Bev on the front desk, who knows every regular’s car by its reg.
