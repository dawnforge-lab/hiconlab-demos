My Services

Colour Analysis

Find the colours that make you shine. This session identifies your best shades based on your natural features. You’ll receive a personalised palette and learn how to use it when shopping or getting dressed⁠—making your wardrobe more cohesive, flattering, and easy to style.

Learn more →

Wardrobe Edit

Together, we’ll assess your current wardrobe, identify what works, and remove what doesn’t. I’ll show you how to create outfits from what you own and suggest key pieces to fill the gaps⁠—bringing order, confidence, and purpose to your daily dressing routine.

Learn more →

Personal Shopping

Skip the overwhelm and shop with intention. Based on your colours, shape, and style, I’ll help you find versatile pieces that fit and flatter. Whether in-store or online, you’ll discover how to shop smarter and build a wardrobe you’ll actually enjoy wearing.

Learn more →

Online Capsule Wardrobe

Personalised online styling: body shape analysis, defined style identity, and a 15–30 piece capsule with 40+ outfits, shoppable links, and 3 weeks’ support — for a wardrobe that works anywhere.

Learn more →

Wardrobe Edit + Personal Shopping

The complete wardrobe reset. We’ll edit what works, clear out what no longer works, and identify exactly what’s missing, then I’ll research and send you a personalised shopping list with direct links to the right pieces. One session, total clarity.

Learn more →

Colour Party

Colour parties are a fun, relaxed way to explore your best shades with friends. Perfect for birthdays or get-togethers, each guest enjoys personalised guidance, live demos, and plenty of laughter. A vibrant, confidence-boosting experience for everyone.

About my approach

Warm, personal, and paced just for you

My approach is shaped by my own journey of overcoming challenges and embracing fresh starts. Through this, I learned how reconnecting with yourself through colour and style can boost confidence and reveal your true identity.

For me, colour and style go beyond appearances — they’re a way to show the world who you really are and to feel empowered every day. When we work together, I listen closely to your story, lifestyle, and dreams to create a look that’s truly you — one that supports and lifts you up.

Let’s find your perfect colours, unique style, and that confident glow you’ve been waiting to shine.

Seasonal colour analysis is a personal styling technique that helps you discover which colours naturally complement your skin tone, hair, and eye colour. It’s based on the four seasons⁠—Spring, Summer, Autumn, and Winter⁠—each representing a unique colour palette. By identifying your season, you can wear colours that enhance your natural features, making you look fresher, healthier, and more radiant.
